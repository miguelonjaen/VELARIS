The distributed motril-pilot.geojson (Desktop: motril-pilot.json) is the complete
derived database. Its source extract, original OSM response, retrieval timestamp,
OSM object versions and reproducible generator are kept in SmartCHARTS/examples/motril
and SmartCHARTS/tools/motril.ts. Changes: selected coastline, pier and breakwater
ways; flattened WGS84 coordinates; stripped unrelated tags; sorted stable IDs.

No surveyed depths, datum conversion, navigation aids or navigational accuracy
are provided. Open coastline ways are retained as lines; no land/water topology
is inferred. These data are incomplete and are not suitable for navigation.
