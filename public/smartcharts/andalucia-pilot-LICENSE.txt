The complete distributed derived database is andalucia-pilot.json. It contains
a simplified overview and full-detail sectors; sector bounds follow longitude
bands and are not administrative provincial borders.

Changes: coastline, pier and breakwater selection; coastline clipping to OSM
Andalusia relation 349044; whole structures retained when intersecting that boundary;
nearby coastal structures retained using an approximate
5 km inclusion filter; stable IDs; metadata; overview-only simplification.
The source extract and all processing instructions are documented in
SmartCHARTS/examples/andalucia. No surveyed depths, contours, verified navigation
aids, hydrological completeness or navigational accuracy are provided.
